# System overview

**PromoGPT-Agent** is an open-source AI automation infrastructure platform for marketing and business operations. It targets businesses, agencies, startups, and enterprises that need autonomous workflows across content, channels, analytics, and orchestration.

## What users can do

- Connect social platforms.
- Bring their own AI providers (BYO keys) or use platform-managed AI.
- Automate content creation and publishing.
- Analyze engagement data.
- Orchestrate durable, event-driven workflows.
- Generate media where the roadmap supports it.
- Publish across channels.
- Embed automation into partner products via API and SDK.

## Product principles

- **API-first** — external systems integrate without UI coupling.
- **Plugin-driven** — extensibility via connectors, workflows, and marketplace (roadmap).
- **Self-hostable** — enterprises own infrastructure, providers, data, and workflows.
- **Enterprise-ready** — deployment flexibility, governance, and security on the roadmap.
- **AI-provider agnostic** — multiple models and endpoints behind an abstraction layer (LiteLLM target).

## Supported deployment models

- **Hosted SaaS** — operated by PromoGPT-Agent; quickest path for SMEs and creators.
- **Self-hosted enterprise** — customer-operated stacks (Docker, Kubernetes, Helm) for regulated or large orgs.

Details: [deployment-models](./deployment-models.md).

## Vertical focus

Marketing automation is the **initial wedge**. Planned expansion includes sales automation, customer support agents, ecommerce operations, internal copilots, and broader workflow ecosystems.

## Long-term direction

Establish PromoGPT-Agent as an **open infrastructure layer** for AI-native marketing and operations: reusable agents, scalable orchestration, embeddable capabilities, and a standard pattern for SaaS builders and enterprises alike.
