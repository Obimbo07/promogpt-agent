# Pricing and revenue

PromoGPT-Agent targets **hybrid** commercial packaging: SaaS subscriptions plus usage-aligned AI economics and enterprise licensing.

## SaaS tiers (V1 framing)

### Free

- For developers and evaluation.
- Limited workflows, AI executions, and social integrations; community support.

### Pro

- Creators and small businesses.
- Advanced workflows, multi-platform publishing, analytics, AI generation, premium integrations.
- Monthly and annual billing.

### Team

- Agencies and growing teams.
- Team workspaces, collaboration, approvals, advanced analytics.
- Priced per workspace (monthly or annual).

### Enterprise

- Enterprises and substantial self-hosted customers.
- Self-hosted entitlement, white-label options, SAML/SSO, SLAs, audit logging, bespoke integrations.
- Custom annual contracts, infrastructure licensing, support retainers.

## AI usage billing

Two complementary options:

### Option A — Bring your own keys (BYO)

Users attach OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or compatible custom endpoints.

**Benefits:** lower platform variable cost, customer control, compatibility with procurement rules.

### Option B — Platform-managed AI

Usage-based billing for managed access: tokens, workflow executions, media generation, compute.

## Metering axes

Track and expose usage for quotas and invoices:

- Prompt and completion tokens.
- Image and video generation where applicable.
- Workflow executions.

Users should see consumption, caps, credits, and plan upgrades where productized.

## Revenue streams

1. SaaS subscriptions (monthly and annual).
2. AI token / usage billing for managed inference.
3. Enterprise licensing for self-hosted deployments.
4. White-label licensing for agency or OEM-style deployments.
5. Marketplace commissions on plugins and integrations (longer horizon).
